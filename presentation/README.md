# Research presentation

The editable presentation is authored with the Presentations plugin in a connected artifact session. Its research figures are based on the frozen CSV outputs in `results/` and the publication figures in `figures/`.

Saved presentation Page: `page_7b0956623db081918a271184f17859e2`.

The final deliverable is output/usgs_authoritative_networks.pptx, with 24 slides and editable charts. slide_data.json and build_slides.mjs preserve its data and authoring logic. The local PowerPoint replaces the unfinished cloud draft following service timeouts. The final comparison includes 25,494 earthquakes from the 12 U.S. authoritative regional networks listed in METHODS.md.
